01Scope and design stance

Specification §1 – §3

In 2022 a multi-vendor team demonstrated at the HIMSS Interoperability Showcase that software could watch a patient's record during an ED visit, recognise the pattern of possible child sex trafficking, administer a validated screen, and route an alert to a social worker in near real time. The demonstration worked. Its limitation was commercial: every component was proprietary, so any hospital adopting the workflow inherited a stack of licences.

Harper keeps the clinical model and the interoperability-first philosophy and replaces each proprietary component with a permissively licensed equivalent built on published standards — FHIR R4 / US Core, CDS Hooks, SMART on FHIR, Structured Data Capture, and the BPM+ Health stack of BPMN, DMN and CQL. The executable clinical process stays modelled in open-standard BPMN orchestrating open-standard DMN decisions: the 2022 design intent, carried forward on an open engine.

Everything in this document describes a system that assists. The screening decision is deterministic and inspectable, a referral is drafted by automation but routed only by a person, and the system never contacts law enforcement on its own.

Automation needs to complement the existing safeguards. Design stance carried forward from the original architect

What is out of scope in this version

  • Adult and labour-trafficking screening — adults use the RAFT tool; both are on the extensibility roadmap.
  • Automated notification to law enforcement. This is deliberately a governed, human-initiated decision, never an automated action.
  • A production predictive model as the basis for referral. Any machine-learning layer is an advisory triage aid only.
  • EHR-vendor-specific certification and go-live procedures for a named hospital.

02Architecture principles

Specification §4 — every design choice below answers to these

Harper is an open-standards system by deliberate design. Every layer — clinical data, real-time decision support, process orchestration, decision logic, screening apps, runtime platform — rests on a published standard and a permissively licensed implementation. No proprietary runtime and no per-seat licence is required to detect. This is the single most important departure from the 2022 blueprint, and it governs everything that follows.

Open and licence-free
Every layer is Apache-2.0, MIT, or the PostgreSQL licence. Terminology carries its own nuances — see §06.
Standards-first interoperability
Loosely coupled services communicate only through published HL7 and OMG standards. No private interfaces anywhere.
Explainable, human-in-the-loop
The screening decision is deterministic and auditable. The system advises; a clinician or social worker decides.
Trauma-informed and victim-centred
Screening is private, respectful, and never conducted in the presence of an accompanying person who could be a trafficker.
Privacy by design
Data minimisation, least privilege, encryption, and strict role-based access to flags and referrals — with attention to minor confidentiality and 42 CFR Part 2.
Portable and boundaryless
Ships as containers; runs on managed cloud, on-premises, or a single node at the edge for sites with poor connectivity.
Extensible
The same pattern generalises to adult, labour, and elder-abuse screening, and to EMS and skilled-nursing settings.

04Runtime view

Specification §6.2 — one BPMN process instance per ED encounter

The pathway mirrors the patient's journey through the department. Each clinical step is a BPMN user task, so an instance runs until it reaches one and then waits for a human to complete it with data; the business-rule tasks read those answers and bind to the DMN decisions. Two BPMN signals link the lanes — the nurse's at-risk decision throws Referral drafted, which starts the practitioner; the practitioner's finalisation throws Suspect sex trafficking, which starts the social worker.

Alternate and exception flows

  • Not eligible. Age outside the validated band, or no high-risk chief complaint — no prompt, and nothing added to the record beyond normal care.
  • Prompt declined. The nurse dismisses the card, for instance because the patient is clinically unstable or not alone. The reason is captured and the prompt can re-fire later in the encounter.
  • Patient accompanied. Screening is deferred until the patient can be seen alone. The system must never surface trafficking content where an accompanying person can see it.
  • Screen negative, clinician concerned. A provider can raise a referral manually. A negative screen never blocks clinical judgment.
  • Prior-visit signal. History indicating a screen should have fired at an earlier visit is surfaced at this one.
  • Connectivity loss. At edge sites the screen still runs locally; referrals queue and reconcile when connectivity returns.

05Decision logic

Specification §8 — three DMN models, no hand-written business logic

All process and decision logic lives in the BPMN and DMN files. Kogito generates one REST endpoint per executable process and per decision; the service contains no hand-written Java. A rule changes by editing a model, never by writing code — which is also what makes the logic portable to another open engine and shareable with another institution.

The three decisions
DecisionFormQuestion it answersInputs
EDPrescreenDMN 1.3 decision tableShould this patient be screened at all?Standing order · high-risk chief complaint · age 13–17
GreenbaumScreenDMN 1.3 literal expressionIs the patient at risk? Positive at ≥ 2 of six items.Seven inputs covering six scored items — all required on every call
AlarmSignsDMN 1.3 count + thresholdDo the clinician's holistic findings warrant suspicion?Seven observed signs, counted against a configurable threshold

Two scorings, one call

The significant-trauma item is answered by two subquestions: 1a broken bones or cuts needing stitches, and 1b knocked unconscious. That is why six scored items take seven inputs. The original screen counts a yes to either subquestion as one positive; a modified tool evaluated in the multi-site study drops 1a and scores 1b alone. greenbaum.dmn publishes both results from a single evaluation, and Harper acts on atRisk — the modified scoring.

The dual scoring is implemented in the decision model and documented in the repository; specification v1.2 §10.4 still describes the earlier single-output form. All seven inputs are required on every call, including historyOfBrokenBonesOrCuts, even though the modified tool does not score it.

Screen performance at the ≥ 2 cutoff — Greenbaum VJ et al., J Adolesc Health 2018 (n = 810, 16 sites; ED subgroup n = 91)

atRisk — Harper default1b only83.3%59.5%84.4%64.6%

Size the social-work path for a high false-positive rate

Dropping item 1a buys roughly ten points of ED specificity at no cost to sensitivity, which is why Harper scores it by default. Even so, at 59.5% specificity roughly forty per cent of non-victims screen positive. The original single-site study reported 92.3% / 74.4% — better than either multi-site result. Do not plan around it. Capacity planning for downstream social work should assume the multi-site figures.

On “metaheuristics” and machine learning

The 2022 blueprint described a metaheuristic risk score computed from accumulating data points. Harper separates two concerns deliberately. The screening decision that can trigger a referral stays deterministic and transparent — DMN and CQL — for clinical safety, auditability and defensibility.

An optional machine-learning layer may help triage, for example by learning which chief-complaint patterns warrant a prompt. It is advisory only, must surface the features behind a suggestion, and never becomes the sole basis for a referral.

Why Kogito

Kogito is the Apache-2.0, cloud-native successor to jBPM and Drools, aligned with the DMN and BPM+ Health heritage of the original design and a natural fit for Quarkus on Kubernetes. Flowable CE and Operaton — the community fork of Camunda 7 — remain drop-in alternatives that run the same model files. Camunda 7 CE itself reached end of life in October 2025, and Camunda 8 is not offered as a free product.

07Deployment view

Specification §10.3 — containerised services on any conformant Kubernetes

Each component ships as an OCI image and runs as a Deployment behind a Service. An Ingress or Gateway exposes the SMART apps and the CDS Hooks endpoint; ConfigMaps and Secrets hold thresholds, routing and credentials; PostgreSQL backs the FHIR server as a StatefulSet or a managed database; and a HorizontalPodAutoscaler scales the stateless decision service to departmental peaks. The stack installs with Helm or Kustomize.

Same manifests, three footprints

A site chooses its footprint without changing the application. What differs is where the trust boundary sits and whether the cluster can reach anything outside it.

Audit and provenance

Every automatically created or modified clinical resource carries a Provenance record, and every access to trafficking-related data and every CDS invocation writes an AuditEvent. Audit records are immutable and retained per institutional policy. A result references the exact version of the decision logic that produced it, so a flag raised eighteen months ago can still be reconstructed against the rules that were live at the time.

09Governance guardrails

Harper screens minors and can trigger a referral. Several of the constraints below are architectural, not procedural: they are enforced by the shape of the system, and removing them would require changing the models rather than changing a policy document.

Licensing — read before deploying

The repository is split-licensed. Everything Project Harper authored — the BPMN processes, the Kogito service, the DMN model structure, the documentation — is Apache-2.0. The clinical content encoded in the Greenbaum decision model is not Harper's to license: it derives from the Short Screen for Child Sex Trafficking, and its redistribution terms have not been established.

Assume permission is required. Obtain your own licensing determination from the rights holder before any clinical or commercial use, and do not treat the repository as authority that you may use the instrument. Whether encoding the items in DMN constitutes a permitted reproduction or a derivative work has not been reviewed by counsel. Status: unresolved and under review. Nothing here is legal advice.

Open questions carried into the build

  • Which reference EHR to target first for the prototype — Epic, Oracle Health, or an open EHR such as OpenMRS.
  • Kogito DMN invocation style: a bare decision, as today, or an exposed DMN Decision Service — and the concrete process data model and typing that goes with it.
  • Consent model specifics per target jurisdiction and institutional policy.
  • Scope and governance of the optional ML triage aid for a first release.
  • A partial-screen path. Today every DMN input is required on every call and a missing field returns HTTP 500 rather than a validation error; adding one means defaulting at the API boundary or returning a 400.
